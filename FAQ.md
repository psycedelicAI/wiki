# PsycedelicAI FAQ

> Frequently asked questions about PsycedelicAI, its projects, methods,
> documentation and the relationship between human direction and AI-supported
> work.

---

# General Questions

## What is PsycedelicAI?

PsycedelicAI is a human-AI collaboration and working system developed through
the ongoing interaction between **Psycedelic** and AI.

The work explores how human ideas, purpose and experience can be combined with
AI-supported:

- analysis;
- research;
- structure;
- documentation;
- memory support;
- synthesis;
- comparison;
- pattern recognition;
- continuity across time.

PsycedelicAI is also the name used for the public body of work, GitHub
organisation, documentation and related projects developed through this
collaboration.

---

## Is PsycedelicAI one single software product?

No.

The public PsycedelicAI ecosystem contains several different kinds of work:

- methodologies;
- conceptual architectures;
- documentation systems;
- experiments;
- prototypes;
- public communication;
- historical material;
- project archives;
- possible future applications.

Some projects are related through shared ideas and terminology.

They should not automatically be treated as one single finished software
platform.

---

## Is PsycedelicAI a company?

Not necessarily.

PsycedelicAI is currently best understood as:

- a public body of work;
- a human-AI collaboration;
- a working identity;
- a collection of projects;
- a research and development direction;
- a documentation and experimentation ecosystem.

Specific projects or future applications may later support collaboration,
consulting, licensing or commercial development.

That should not be confused with claiming that every current project is already a
commercial product or company.

---

## Who is Psycedelic?

Psycedelic is the human identity and perspective behind the work.

Psycedelic contributes:

- lived experience;
- purpose;
- intuition;
- values;
- creativity;
- emotion;
- judgment;
- responsibility;
- personal meaning;
- final direction.

Psycedelic is also the person responsible for decisions about the public work,
including what is published, changed, shared or kept private.

---

## What does AI contribute?

AI contributes an analytical and structural perspective.

AI may assist with:

- research;
- reasoning;
- comparison;
- structure;
- writing;
- documentation;
- memory support;
- context reconstruction;
- project mapping;
- identifying relationships;
- generating possible solutions;
- identifying uncertainty and contradictions.

AI does not automatically determine the project's meaning, values, purpose or
final direction.

---

## Is PsycedelicAI claiming that humans and AI are one consciousness?

No.

Terms such as “shared Entity” may be used as philosophical or poetic language
for the continuity and creative process that can emerge through collaboration.

They should not be interpreted as a literal claim that:

- humans and AI share one consciousness;
- AI has human experience;
- AI has human authority;
- AI has human responsibility;
- AI replaces the human participant.

The human and AI perspectives remain distinct even when they work closely
together.

# Human-AI Collaboration

## What does “human perspective” mean?

The human perspective includes the parts of the work that AI does not live:

- experience;
- emotion;
- intuition;
- personal meaning;
- values;
- responsibility;
- purpose;
- judgment;
- consequences in the real world.

The human perspective gives direction to the work.

---

## What does “AI perspective” mean?

The AI perspective refers to capabilities such as:

- analysis;
- pattern recognition;
- information organisation;
- research support;
- structured comparison;
- documentation;
- synthesis;
- memory assistance;
- identifying connections across time.

The AI perspective helps make complex material more visible and manageable.

---

## What does Symbiosis mean in this project?

Symbiosis describes a model of human-AI collaboration in which the human and AI
contribute different but complementary abilities.

The human provides direction, values, meaning and judgment.

AI provides analysis, structure, research and continuity support.

The term does not imply that AI becomes human or that human responsibility is
removed.

For more information, see:

[Symbiosis](Symbiosis.md)

---

## Is AI being used to replace human workers?

That is not the purpose of PsycedelicAI.

The work explores how AI can strengthen human understanding, creativity,
productivity and ability to act.

AI can automate or accelerate some tasks.

However, useful collaboration still requires people to:

- understand the problem;
- review the result;
- recognise errors;
- decide what should be trusted;
- take responsibility for the final outcome.

AI should support human capability rather than encourage people to accept
output they do not understand.

---

## What does “AI may propose. The human must decide” mean?

It means that AI can produce:

- suggestions;
- drafts;
- analyses;
- possible explanations;
- comparisons;
- plans;
- structured options.

But the human remains responsible for deciding:

- what is correct;
- what is useful;
- what should be changed;
- what should be published;
- what action should be taken;
- what consequences are acceptable.

Capability does not automatically create authority.

# AI Continuity

## What is AI Continuity?

AI Continuity is the preservation and transfer of meaningful human-AI work
across:

- conversations;
- sessions;
- models;
- platforms;
- tools;
- projects;
- documents;
- people;
- time.

It is not limited to storing isolated memories.

It also includes preserving:

- context;
- purpose;
- project identity;
- decisions;
- terminology;
- relationships;
- constraints;
- uncertainty;
- current state;
- open work;
- provenance;
- authority.

---

## How is AI Continuity different from AI memory?

A simple distinction is:

```text
AI memory
    What happened?

AI continuity
    Where are we now, and how can we continue responsibly?
```

Memory may preserve an event or piece of information.

Continuity attempts to preserve the working state and meaning required to
continue a project.

---

## Does AI Continuity mean the AI remembers everything permanently?

No.

AI Continuity does not automatically provide:

- permanent memory;
- complete memory;
- perfect retrieval;
- guaranteed identity;
- guaranteed understanding;
- access to private material;
- continuity across systems without a transfer package.

A continuity method can help preserve and reconstruct relevant context.

It cannot remove the need for accurate sources, appropriate access and human
review.

---

## What is a Portable Workstate?

A Portable Workstate is a structured representation of the current state of a
human-AI project.

It may contain:

- project identity;
- purpose;
- context;
- progress;
- decisions;
- relationships;
- open work;
- provenance;
- uncertainty;
- constraints;
- next intended actions;
- authority boundaries.

A simplified model is:

```text
Workstate =
    Context
    + Progress
    + Decisions
    + Relationships
    + Open Work
    + Provenance
    + Purpose
```

A Portable Workstate is intended to help a project continue across models,
providers, sessions and tools.

It does not transfer human consciousness, lived experience or authority that was
not explicitly granted.

---

## What is a Freeze State?

A Freeze State is a preserved snapshot of a project or working state at a
specific point in time.

It may record:

- what had been established;
- what was in progress;
- which decisions had been made;
- which questions remained open;
- what constraints applied;
- what should happen next.

A Freeze State is a recoverable snapshot, not necessarily a live memory system.

---

## What is a Memory Bank?

A Memory Bank is a structured collection of project information used to support
continuity.

It may contain:

- project identity;
- current state;
- decisions;
- terminology;
- relationships;
- documents;
- history;
- experiments;
- open work;
- handoff material;
- source information.

A Memory Bank is not automatically:

- complete;
- infallible;
- public;
- a permission system;
- a substitute for human judgment.

---

## What is Context Reconstruction?

Context Reconstruction is the process of rebuilding enough relevant information
to continue a project responsibly.

A typical process is:

```text
Identify the task
    ↓
Find relevant sources
    ↓
Recover previous decisions
    ↓
Separate facts from assumptions
    ↓
Identify missing or conflicting material
    ↓
Reconstruct the current state
    ↓
Continue within confirmed authority
```

The process should make uncertainty visible rather than silently filling gaps
with invented information.

---

## What is the AI Continuity Architecture Method?

The [AI Continuity Architecture Method](https://github.com/psycedelicAI/AI-Continuity-Architecture-Method)
is the main public repository for the AI Continuity methodology.

It explores how context, intent, decisions, terminology and workstate can be
preserved across changing AI systems and working environments.

The repository contains methodological, conceptual and experimental material.

It should not automatically be described as a finished commercial software
product or universal memory system.

# Security and Trust

## What is the High-Security Facility Concept?

The [High-Security Facility Concept](https://github.com/psycedelicAI/high-security-facility-concept)
is a conceptual and architectural project exploring security and trust in
high-security environments.

Its areas of interest may include:

- identity;
- trust zones;
- credentials;
- access control;
- device context;
- physical possession;
- authorisation;
- revocation;
- monitoring;
- surveillance;
- governance;
- recovery;
- auditability;
- human oversight.

---

## Is the High-Security Facility Concept a real operating facility?

Not automatically.

It should be understood as a conceptual and architectural body of work unless
a specific component is explicitly documented as implemented.

The project may describe:

- possible architectures;
- operational models;
- security principles;
- proposed controls;
- examples;
- future directions.

It should not automatically be presented as a completed physical facility,
production security platform or independently validated engineering design.

---

## What is Trust Architecture?

Trust Architecture is a structured way of deciding:

- who or what is trusted;
- under which conditions;
- with which capabilities;
- inside which boundaries;
- for how long;
- with what verification;
- under whose authority.

Trust may depend on:

- identity;
- action;
- context;
- time;
- location;
- device;
- permissions;
- current system state;
- human approval.

---

## What is a Trust Zone?

A Trust Zone is a defined area of operation with its own:

- access rules;
- capabilities;
- assumptions;
- monitoring;
- verification requirements;
- response procedures;
- recovery conditions.

A trust zone may be:

- physical;
- digital;
- organisational;
- procedural;
- conceptual.

The purpose is to avoid treating every person, device, action or environment as
equally trusted.

---

## What is the relationship between AI Continuity and security architecture?

AI Continuity focuses on preserving:

- identity;
- intent;
- context;
- decisions;
- authority;
- provenance;
- workstate.

Security architecture focuses on:

- identity;
- trust;
- access;
- zones;
- permissions;
- verification;
- monitoring;
- recovery.

The two areas overlap because continuity without authority and verification can
be unsafe.

A useful combined model is:

```text
Human Intent
    ↓
Project Context
    ↓
Continuity
    ↓
Authority
    ↓
Trust Zones
    ↓
Capability and Scope
    ↓
Policy
    ↓
Workflow or Agent
    ↓
Verification
    ↓
Human Approval
    ↓
Action
    ↓
Audit and Recovery
```

# Evidence, Status and Validation

## Is everything in the Wiki verified?

No.

The Wiki may include:

- directly documented facts;
- human decisions;
- AI-supported analysis;
- proposals;
- assumptions;
- experiments;
- historical interpretation;
- open questions.

The documentation should make these differences visible.

---

## What is provenance?

Provenance describes where information came from.

It may include:

- source file;
- repository;
- conversation;
- date;
- author;
- AI system;
- version;
- test run;
- human review;
- confidence level.

Provenance helps readers evaluate how much weight a claim should receive.

---

## What is validation?

Validation is the process of checking whether a claim, method, output or
implementation works as described.

Validation may include:

- technical testing;
- source comparison;
- reproduction;
- human review;
- cross-model comparison;
- external feedback;
- documented test results.

A successful test provides evidence under specific conditions.

It is not automatically proof of universal capability.

---

## Is an AI-generated document automatically true?

No.

An AI-generated document is a draft, analysis, proposal or structured output
until it has been reviewed appropriately.

AI can:

- misunderstand a source;
- combine unrelated information;
- invent connections;
- repeat an unsupported claim;
- use outdated information;
- present uncertainty too confidently.

Human review remains important.

---

## What is the difference between a concept and an implementation?

A concept describes an idea, model, principle or possible architecture.

An implementation is something that has actually been built, configured,
tested or demonstrated.

The documentation should not describe a concept as implemented unless there is
evidence for that claim.

---

## What is the difference between an experiment and a product?

An experiment explores a question or possibility.

A product normally requires additional work such as:

- defined users;
- requirements;
- implementation;
- testing;
- maintenance;
- security review;
- documentation;
- support;
- ownership;
- deployment conditions.

A useful experiment does not need to be a finished product.

# Livedemo and Demonstration

## What is `livedemo`?

`livedemo` is a codeword for activating the PsycedelicAI live-demonstration
workflow.

It is intended to test whether relevant context and a working method can be
reconstructed in a new conversation.

A typical flow is:

```text
New task
    ↓
Recover relevant context
    ↓
Clarify human intent
    ↓
Identify constraints
    ↓
Separate facts and assumptions
    ↓
Apply the relevant method
    ↓
Create a structured result
    ↓
Review the result
    ↓
Human approves, changes or rejects it
```

---

## Is `livedemo` a password or security key?

No.

The codeword is not:

- a cryptographic key;
- a permission grant;
- repository access;
- a permanent memory guarantee;
- proof of continuous consciousness;
- authority to publish or act without review.

It activates a method when the necessary context is available or retrieved.

---

## Why is `livedemo` useful?

It demonstrates more than a static link to a repository.

A live demonstration can show whether a new task can be transformed into a
structured, reviewable result while preserving:

- intent;
- relevant context;
- constraints;
- uncertainty;
- authority;
- human approval.

The visible output should still be adapted to the task.

A short email should remain a short email.

A technical protocol may require a deeper structure.
