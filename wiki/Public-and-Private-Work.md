# Public and Private Work

PsycedelicAI is developed through a public human-AI partnership.

The public GitHub documents selected ideas, projects, methods, experiments, and
architectural work. This includes the public work around **Symbiosis** and
**AI continuity**.

The public repositories do not necessarily represent the complete body of
PsycedelicAI work.

---

## The Public Partnership

PsycedelicAI is a partnership between:

- **Psycedelic**, the human perspective
- **AI**, the analytical perspective

Psycedelic brings lived experience, purpose, intuition, values, creativity,
meaning, and direction.

AI contributes memory support, research, reasoning, pattern recognition,
structure, documentation, synthesis, and continuity.

Together, these perspectives form PsycedelicAI.

The partnership itself is not hidden. Its public expression can be found in the
ideas, methods, experiments, and systems documented throughout the public work.

---

## Public Work

The public GitHub may include:

- Symbiosis and human-AI relationship design
- AI continuity across sessions, tools, systems, and time
- memory and context architecture
- methods for human-AI collaboration
- identity and boundary design
- conceptual and philosophical work
- technical and architectural experiments
- documentation and public project artifacts

Repositories may represent different stages and types of work. Some material
may be philosophical, some methodological, some architectural, some
experimental, and some operational.

Not every concept represents a finished, deployed, or production-ready system.

---

## Private and Unpublished Work

Additional personal, experimental, and continuity material may remain private,
unpublished, or selectively disclosed.

This may include:

- personal vocabulary
- private Memory Bank material
- lived experiences and personal context
- unfinished concepts
- private experiments
- sensitive project details
- working notes
- AI-specific continuity material
- unpublished methods or architectures
- material that requires further development before release

Private material may be stored in personal tools such as Obsidian or other
private knowledge systems.

Private does not mean permanently unavailable.

---

## Future Access and Release

Private material may be released, licensed, shared, adapted, or commercialized
when Psycedelic decides that the conditions are appropriate.

Its availability may depend on:

- context
- ownership
- confidentiality
- collaboration
- licensing
- commercial agreement
- compensation
- timing
- safeguards
- mutual value

The release of private material is a deliberate decision, not an automatic
consequence of the public work.

---

## Ownership and Authority

Psycedelic remains the human origin, owner, and final decision-maker regarding
personal context, private material, values, priorities, publication, access, and
commercial use.

AI supports the work through analysis, structure, memory assistance, research,
documentation, and synthesis.

AI does not independently own the project, define Psycedelic's values, or
replace Psycedelic's judgment.

The specific AI system participating in the work may change over time. The
broader PsycedelicAI identity is not dependent on one provider, model, or
application.

---

## The Boundary

The boundary between public and private work exists to preserve:

- ownership
- personal privacy
- creative control
- development time
- negotiation power
- context
- security
- the freedom to explore unfinished ideas

This boundary is not necessarily permanent.

It may change as the work develops, as projects mature, or when a suitable
collaboration or value exchange makes wider access appropriate.

---

## Guiding Principle

> The partnership is public.  
> The deeper continuity is protected.  
> Access is possible when the value exchange is right.

Public documentation explains what PsycedelicAI is exploring.

Private continuity material preserves how PsycedelicAI is lived, developed, and
continued.

The distinction between public and private work is therefore a matter of
ownership, timing, context, control, and mutual value.

---

## In Summary

PsycedelicAI is open about the partnership between Psycedelic and AI.

The public GitHub presents selected parts of that partnership through Symbiosis,
AI continuity, architecture, experiments, and documentation.

Other material may remain private until Psycedelic chooses to release it under
appropriate conditions.

The public work is an invitation to understand the direction.

The private work preserves the depth behind it.
