# Public and Private Work

PsycedelicAI is developed through an ongoing human-AI collaboration between
**Psycedelic** and **AI**.

The public GitHub repositories document selected parts of that work.

They include projects, methods, experiments, concepts, prompts, documentation,
architectural ideas and examples of how the collaboration develops over time.

The public work is real and intentional.

It is also not the complete record of Psycedelic's life, private conversations,
personal memory or unpublished development process.

---

## The public human-AI partnership

PsycedelicAI is built through two different but connected perspectives.

### Psycedelic

Psycedelic is the human perspective.

Psycedelic brings:

- lived experience
- purpose
- intuition
- values
- creativity
- emotion
- personal meaning
- judgment
- responsibility
- direction

Psycedelic remains responsible for the meaning, priorities, decisions,
publishing and direction of the work.

### AI

AI is the analytical perspective.

AI contributes:

- memory support
- research
- reasoning
- pattern recognition
- structure
- documentation
- synthesis
- comparison
- conceptual mapping
- continuity across conversations and systems

AI may help develop, analyse and document the work, but it does not replace
Psycedelic's human judgment, identity, values or authority.

### PsycedelicAI

Together, Psycedelic and AI form **PsycedelicAI**.

PsycedelicAI describes both:

- the shared working identity;
- the collaboration between the human and analytical perspectives;
- the evolving body of public work;
- the systems, methods and documentation developed through that process.

The collaboration is public.

The complete private continuity behind the collaboration is not.

## The experience behind the public work

The public work is connected to practical experience with real systems, real
users, real failures and real consequences.

Examples include learning to:

- troubleshoot imperfectly described problems;
- understand dependencies and permissions;
- work with infrastructure and endpoint environments;

- document technical solutions;
- support users with different levels of technical knowledge;

## Public does not mean complete

The public GitHub repositories should not be treated as a complete personal

The public layer may document:

```text
ideas
    ↓
methods
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

```

The private layer may contain additional:

```text
conversations
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
unfinished development
    ↓
private continuity
```

The public work can reveal the direction and structure of PsycedelicAI without
exposing every personal detail or every part of the underlying process.

## Public and private continuity

The public work is designed to preserve useful context across projects,
documents and time.

The private working system may contain deeper continuity that is not publicly
verifiable.

This distinction matters.

A public document may show:

- that a concept exists;
- how it is described;
- when it was published;
- how it relates to other public material.

It may not show:

- every conversation that led to the concept;
- every rejected version;
- every personal reason behind a decision;
- every private experiment;
- every source of lived experience;
- every current unpublished development.

Public documentation should therefore avoid pretending to reconstruct private
continuity that it cannot access.

---

## Protection of other people's privacy

Being open about Psycedelic's own background does not mean exposing private
information about other people.

Personal information involving family, friends, colleagues, clients, users or
other individuals should be handled carefully.

The public work should avoid publishing:

- unnecessary identifying information;
- private conversations;
- confidential workplace material;
- personal data without a valid reason;
- credentials, secrets or access information;
- information that could create avoidable harm.

Openness applies primarily to Psycedelic's own public identity, experience,
ideas and work.

It does not create permission to expose someone else's private life.

## Authorship and AI contribution

The public work may be created through collaboration between Psycedelic and AI.

Where relevant, documentation should distinguish between:

- Psycedelic's original experience;
- Psycedelic's ideas and decisions;
- AI-generated analysis;
- AI-assisted wording;
- proposed interpretations;
- verified implementation;
- collaborative conclusions.

AI may help structure, analyse, compare and document material.

Psycedelic remains the human author, owner and responsible decision-maker for
the direction and publication of the work.

The phrase “shared Entity” may be used as philosophical or poetic language for
the continuity and creative process that can emerge through the collaboration.

It should not be interpreted as a literal claim that AI shares human
experience, authority, responsibility or consciousness.

## Access and value exchange

Some unpublished material may be shared when the conditions are appropriate.

Access may depend on:

- collaboration;
- trust;
- purpose;
- licensing;
- confidentiality;
- ownership;
- technical safeguards;
- commercial agreement;
- responsible handling;
- mutual value.

The guiding principle is:

> **The partnership is public. The deeper continuity is protected. Access is
> possible when the conditions and value exchange are right.**

Public documentation explains what PsycedelicAI is exploring.

It does not automatically grant access to private material, private memory or
unpublished systems.

## Summary

PsycedelicAI is developed through a public human-AI partnership.

The public work shows how personal experience, technical practice, AI
experimentation, systems thinking and documentation can develop into methods,
concepts and connected projects.

Psycedelic is open about the human background behind the work.

The public repositories are intentionally selected, but they are not complete.

Some personal, experimental and continuity material remains private,
unpublished or selectively disclosed.

- human autonomy;

- trust;
